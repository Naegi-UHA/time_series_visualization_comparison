Pairwise Comparison Graph

Pairwise comparison graph will appear here.

- This interactive tool compares time-series datasets and helps users explore their similarities.

- Use the left panel to upload datasets and select the comparison method.

- The middle panel displays the comparison results.

- The right panel will show the steps involved in the calculation.

- Each dataset is represented using the selected strategy (for example, one barycenter or medoid per dimension), then the selected distance is averaged across all pairs of representatives. Pairwise mean instead compares the original series directly. The resulting distances fill a symmetric matrix; blue indicates smaller distances and red indicates larger ones.
