Quick guide

Compare : Upload .ts, .txt or .arff files and select at least two datasets. Use Select method to choose a representative strategy and distance; set window or slope when available, then click Compare selected sets.

Explore : The Dataset Map shows datasets and pairwise distances; the Heat Map shows the distance matrix. Change its color gradient, zoom with the mouse wheel, or drag to pan. Click a map point to view a dataset's series; choose its class, series and dimension, hover for values, and toggle Z-normalization.

Animation : Choose any two compared datasets in the right panel. The colored curves illustrate their first series and dimension; the displayed distance is the actual full-pair result. Click Replay animation to replay it.

More : Rename or recolor datasets, toggle their selection, export CSV/ODS/PDF/LaTeX, clear the comparison cache, or switch between light and dark themes.
