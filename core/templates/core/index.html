Pairwise Comparison Graph

Pairwise comparison graph will appear here.

- This interactive tool compares time-series datasets and helps users explore their similarities.

- Use the left panel to upload datasets and select the comparison method.

- The middle panel displays the comparison results.

- The right panel will show the steps involved in the calculation.

- The heatmap uses the selected method to calculate pairwise dataset distances, averaging the distances between their time series. Each distance is shown symmetrically in the matrix; blue indicates smaller distances and red indicates larger ones.
